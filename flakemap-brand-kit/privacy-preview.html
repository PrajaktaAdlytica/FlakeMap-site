CONCEPT PREVIEW · DEMO / SYNTHETIC DATA · No connected services

Local prototype notice / 8 October 2026

A clear boundary.

This notice describes this local FlakeMap concept preview. It is not a published privacy policy for an operating company.

What the forms do

Form entries are used only in browser memory to demonstrate validation, review, and confirmation. The form code makes no network request and uses no cookies or browser storage. Reset clears the entries; closing or reloading the page discards them.

What the demo contains

All workflow records, owners, timestamps, and metrics are fictional fixtures. There is no account authentication, production trace import, external write, billing, or CI connection.

What remains before launch

The legal operator, privacy contact, processor list, retention period, deletion route, consent records, and any analytics policy must be approved and implemented before real lead collection. No contact address has been invented for this preview.
